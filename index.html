Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about CNG kit fitting. Can't find your answer? Call or WhatsApp us.

Is CNG safe for my car?

Yes. CNG has a high auto-ignition temperature (about 540°C) and a narrow flammability range, and it disperses upwards when released, so accidental combustion is far less likely than with petrol. Safety also depends on a properly fitted, approved kit and a tested cylinder, which is why we use certified components.

Is CNG kit fitting legal, and do you handle the RTO paperwork?

Yes. A CNG kit must be fitted by an approved centre and the change recorded on your RC through RTO endorsement. We are a government approved retro-fitment centre and assist with the RTO endorsement.

Which CNG kits do you fit?

We are authorized dealers for leading brands including Zavoli, BRC and Tomasetto, and fit Italian and Indian sequential CNG/LPG kits. Tell us your car model and we will suggest the right kit.

How much does it cost, and can I pay in instalments?

The price depends on your car model and the kit you choose, so call or WhatsApp us with your car details for a quote. We offer 0% down payment options and easy EMI schemes of 3, 6, 9 or 12 months.

Will a CNG kit affect my engine or mileage?

A properly tuned sequential kit lets the engine run smoothly, and CNG is lead and sulphur-free and non-corrosive, which helps spark plug life. Running cost per km is usually lower than petrol. Actual savings depend on your car and driving, and you can estimate them with the savings calculator on this page.

Will I lose boot space?

The cylinder is normally fitted in the boot, so some space is used. The amount depends on your car and the cylinder size. We can show you the options before fitting.

What is cylinder hydro testing and when is it needed?

Hydro testing checks that the CNG cylinder can safely hold pressure. It is required at regular intervals under the rules. We offer cylinder hydro testing, so ask us when yours is due.

Does a CNG kit affect my car warranty or insurance?

This depends on the manufacturer and insurer. Fitting through an approved centre and getting the RTO endorsement is the correct way to keep your records in order. Please also inform your insurer of the change.
