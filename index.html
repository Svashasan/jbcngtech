Why Choose CNG?

Switching to CNG isn't just about saving money; it's about better performance and a cleaner environment.

Eco-Friendly

Commonly referred to as "Green Fuel". Lead and sulphur-free character reduces harmful emissions. Non-corrosive nature increases spark plug longevity.

High Safety

High auto-ignition temperature (540°C) and narrow flammability range makes accidental combustion very unlikely compared to petrol.
